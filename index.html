How it works

1. Enter an email

Type one address, or upload a file with many. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

2. We check the setup

We look at the format, the domain, the mail servers (MX), SPF and DMARC rules, and known disposable or role addresses.

3. Get a clear score

You see Good, Risky or Bad, with a short reason for each check so you know what to do next.

MailCheck checks the domain and email setup only. It cannot confirm that a specific mailbox exists, so treat every score as a helpful guide, not a final answer.
